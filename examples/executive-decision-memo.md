# Executive Decision Memo — Worked Example

## User prompt

> Use $executive-decision-memo to recommend whether our retailer should renew a three-year warehouse automation contract now or run a six-month competitive process. The incumbent offers a 7% discount for signing this month; service levels are acceptable but not excellent.

## Sample output

# Decision Memo: Warehouse Automation Renewal

## Decision required

Approve a six-month competitive process while negotiating a nine-month extension with the incumbent; do not accept the three-year early-renewal offer this month.

## Recommendation

Preserve operational continuity through a short extension and test the market because the 7% discount is worth less than the potential three-year cost and service difference, while an immediate switch would create unacceptable peak-season risk.

## Rationale

1. The discount saves approximately £0.8m over three years, but preliminary benchmarks indicate a £1.5–2.3m range between credible providers.
2. Service is inside contract thresholds yet misses internal recovery-time targets, so renewal would lock in a known performance gap.
3. A nine-month extension moves any transition beyond peak season and creates time for site validation.

## Options

| Option | Economics | Operational risk | When it wins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Renew now | Certain £0.8m saving | Low near-term, locked-in service gap | Market alternatives are not credible |
| Compete with extension | Potential £1.5–2.3m benefit | Controlled | Recommended |
| Switch immediately | Unvalidated | High before peak | Only after severe incumbent failure |

## Risks and controls

| Risk | Mitigation | Revisit trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incumbent refuses extension | Negotiate before launching RFP; maintain renewal fallback | No extension by 15 August |
| Competitor claims are overstated | Require live-site references and a paid pilot | Pilot misses recovery-time target |
| Process consumes management time | Limit to three qualified bidders and four decision criteria | Fewer than two pass prequalification |

## Next actions

Procurement owns extension terms within two weeks; operations defines service gates within ten days; finance validates the total-cost model before RFP issue.

## Why this is a strong response

- It states a decision in the opening lines.
- The alternatives are real and evaluated on decision criteria.
- Each risk has a mitigation and revisit trigger.
