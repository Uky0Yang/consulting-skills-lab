# Market Map Signal Scan — Worked Example

## User prompt

> Use $market-map-signal-scan to assess whether a startup should enter AI quality assurance for contact centers in the UK and Germany. Focus on credible opportunity gaps, not a generic competitor list.

## Sample output

### Executive answer

Enter only around regulated mid-market contact centers that need auditable quality review across human and AI-handled interactions. The broad category is crowded, but evidence is directionally stronger for audit trails, multilingual policy testing, and integration with legacy recording systems than for another general conversation-analytics product.

### Market structure

| Segment | Buyer job | Current alternatives | Basis of competition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise suites | Consolidate workforce and analytics | CCaaS and workforce platforms | Distribution and integration breadth |
| QA specialists | Increase review coverage | Manual sampling and specialist tools | Workflow depth and coaching |
| AI-agent testing | Validate automated interactions | Internal scripts and emerging vendors | Simulation, traceability, speed |
| Regulated mid-market | Prove policy compliance affordably | Manual review and consultants | Trust, auditability, deployment friction |

### Signal register

| Claim | Independent signals | Confidence | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manual sampling is inadequate | Buyer interviews plus QA job growth | Directional | Coverage is a credible pain point |
| Buyers will pay for auditability | Regulated RFP language plus compliance budgets | Directional | Lead with control evidence |
| General analytics is saturated | Dense vendor set plus suite bundling | Proven | Avoid broad category positioning |
| AI-agent testing is urgent now | Vendor launches but limited buying evidence | Weak | Treat as option, not base case |

### Opportunity thesis

Target 200–2,000-seat regulated contact centers with a deployment that reads existing recordings, tests policy adherence in English and German, and produces reviewer-level evidence. Validate through 12 buyer interviews, three data-access pilots, and willingness-to-pay tests before building simulation features.

### Stop criteria

- Buyers require replacement of the recording platform.
- Compliance teams will not accept model-generated evidence without full traceability.
- Fewer than three of twelve buyers fund the problem from an identified budget.

## Why this is a strong response

- It distinguishes market visibility from buyer behavior.
- It includes substitutes, gatekeepers, and constraints.
- The opportunity is expressed as a falsifiable thesis.
