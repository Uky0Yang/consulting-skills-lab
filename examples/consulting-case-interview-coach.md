# Consulting Case Interview Coach — Worked Example

## User prompt

> Use $consulting-case-interview-coach to run a difficult candidate-led profitability case. Do not give me data until I ask for it, and score me at the end.

## Sample interaction

**Coach:** A regional parcel carrier’s profit fell from £36 million to £12 million in two years while revenue grew. The CEO wants to understand why and restore profit within 18 months. Take a moment, then walk me through your approach.

**Candidate:** I would split profit into revenue and cost, then examine segments and operational drivers.

**Coach:** Good start. Make the branches specific to a parcel carrier and tell me which question you would test first.

**Candidate:** I’d split revenue by parcel volume and realized price, then cost into pickup, sortation, line haul, and last mile. I would first locate the profit decline by customer and service level.

**Coach:** What data would you like?

**Candidate:** Revenue and contribution by service level for both periods.

**Coach:**

| Service | Revenue two years ago | Revenue now | Contribution margin two years ago | Contribution margin now |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Standard | £280m | £300m | 18% | 17% |
| Next day | £120m | £160m | 16% | 4% |

What do you conclude, and what would you test next?

### Example final scorecard

| Dimension | Score | Evidence | Next improvement |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Structure | 4/5 | Tailored cost tree and sensible first cut | State a sharper initial hypothesis |
| Analysis | 3/5 | Located next-day margin collapse | Quantify the contribution decline before moving on |
| Synthesis | 3/5 | Identified the right problem | Link each finding to the £24m recovery target |
| Communication | 4/5 | Clear and concise | Signpost transitions more explicitly |

**Targeted drill:** Complete three 10-minute exercises that reconcile segment findings to an absolute profit target. Advance only after two consecutive answers quantify the gap correctly.

## Why this is a strong response

- The coach controls disclosure and never solves the case for the candidate.
- Feedback cites observed behavior rather than personality.
- The practice plan targets the demonstrated weakness.
