# Agentic AI Transformation Office — Worked Example

## User prompt

> Use $agentic-ai-transformation-office to diagnose why our European B2B distributor has 14 AI pilots but no measurable value. Recommend a 90-day reset. We have 3,000 employees, fragmented CRM data, and strict customer-data controls.

## Sample output

### Executive answer

Stop expanding the pilot count. Reset the portfolio around two owned workflows—quote preparation and service-case resolution—and require operational baselines, workflow owners, and control gates before further funding. The current constraint is not model access; it is fragmented ownership, weak data permissions, and adoption measured as usage rather than business performance.

### Portfolio decision

| Use case | Decision | Reason | 90-day success measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quote preparation | Scale conditionally | High sales capacity value; CRM quality is manageable by segment | 20% lower quote cycle time with no margin leakage |
| Service-case resolution | Redesign first | Strong volume; customer-data permissions are unresolved | 15% lower handling time with stable reopen rate |
| Meeting summaries | Stop central funding | Convenience benefit without material enterprise value | Team-funded only |
| Autonomous discount approval | Stop | Irreversible commercial risk and weak controls | Not applicable |

### 30/60/90 reset

- Days 0–30: name workflow and value owners; baseline cycle time, rework, quality, and cost; define permitted data and human approval points.
- Days 31–60: deploy to two sales regions and one service team; run weekly exception review; compare against holdout teams.
- Days 61–90: scale only if operational gates pass; move benefits into the finance tracker; retire duplicate pilots.

### Governance

| Decision | Owner | Cadence | Escalation trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Portfolio funding | COO | Monthly | No credible value owner or baseline |
| Workflow release | Sales/service owner | Fortnightly | Quality below control threshold |
| Data permissions | DPO and security lead | Fortnightly | New data class or external tool access |
| Benefits validation | Finance partner | Monthly | Claimed value cannot be reconciled to operations |

### Evidence still required

Confirm current quote rework, service reopen rates, CRM completeness by segment, and the percentage of time spent on eligible tasks. Until those inputs are validated, value estimates remain directional.

## Why this is a strong response

- It changes portfolio and operating routines instead of recommending more pilots.
- It distinguishes reversible assistance from high-risk delegated decisions.
- It connects adoption to operational and finance-validated outcomes.
