## 3. Scoped findings for the decision

1. **There is a directional workflow benefit to test, not a measured productivity saving.** E1 describes perceived improvement at shift change, while also naming retained exception-list work. Confidence is directional because there is one firsthand qualitative account and no total task-time measure; E3 confirms a reported measurement gap. Determine whether total effort falls after including rework and exceptions.
2. **The ten-hour saving is unverified and has one underlying claim source.** E2 and E4 repeat U1. Repetition does not strengthen its independence. Do not book ten hours per site or multiply it across the five proposed deployment sites. The two speakers do not establish prevalence or transferability.
3. **Cash savings are not demonstrated.** E3 reports unchanged overtime spending. That challenges a cash-saving narrative but does not disprove all time or service benefits. Finance must reconcile comparable demand, staffing, rates, costs and implementation expenses before a net benefit can be stated.
4. **Exception reliability is unresolved and decision-relevant.** E5 reports three missed exceptions during week four, while E1 reports continuing exception rewrites. Confidence is low for incident incidence and causation: the denominator, severity, exact wording and records are absent. Use this as a verification priority, not a site-wide rate or proof of failure.

## 5. Bounded pilot-extension proposal and gate

Propose, subject to sponsor approval, **four additional weekly scheduling cycles at the existing pilot site**, with no new-site deployment during measurement. This is a pragmatic evidence window, not proof of seasonality or long-run transferability. The calendar, staff effort limit and decision thresholds are not yet approved. Set these before starting; if a meaningful baseline cannot be constructed, report the limitation rather than call a before/after difference causal.

| Finance controller | By the end of the four-cycle window: reconcile time records, overtime, implementation/support costs and comparator conditions | Separate gross time released, usable capacity and realizable net cash benefit; maintain unverified items as unverified |
| Sponsor with operations and finance leads | At the window end: deploy, narrow the offer, continue only against a newly authorized gap, or stop | Written decision with evidence IDs, limits and five-site transferability assumptions |

Rollout requires substantiated value on the agreed definition and no unresolved reliability risk beyond the sponsor's agreed tolerance, plus a credible implementation/transferability case for the five additional sites. Any serious verified missed-exception event during an authorized extension triggers immediate process-owner review and a pause in expansion. Inadequate records at the deadline mean the savings case remains unverified; they do not license indefinite pilot continuation or a confident rollout claim.
