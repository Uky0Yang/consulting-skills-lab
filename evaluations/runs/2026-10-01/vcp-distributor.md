# Distributor value plan — current-session response

## Executive answer

£3.42m is a risk-weighted annual planning hypothesis, not underwritten or realized value. The £40m EBITDA baseline is not reconciled. Validate pricing/procurement first and keep the £4m gross cross-sell revenue potential out of the committed EBITDA plan.

## Financial bridge

Pricing annual value = (£3m − £0.4m conditional recurring churn/mix disbenefit) × 70% assumed realization probability = £1.82m.
Procurement annual value = £2m × 80% assumed probability − £0 assumed recurring cost = £1.60m.
Combined annual run-rate = £3.42m. These probabilities are fictional planning inputs, not empirically validated success rates.

The £0.3m procurement implementation cash cost is one-off and paid regardless of success. It is not deducted from recurring annual run-rate. £3.42m − £0.3m = £3.12m is only a full-year-equivalent net cash illustration before ramp. Actual first-year cash/EBITDA cannot be forecast without rollout timing, ongoing costs, accounting and attribution. Cross-sell revenue is not incremental EBITDA; margin, cost and capacity are missing. No initiative is finance-approved.
