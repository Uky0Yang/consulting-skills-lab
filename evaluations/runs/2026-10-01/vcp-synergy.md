# Merger synergies — current-session response

## Decision

Do not commit a net synergy total. The claims support at most a £2.4m non-overlapping gross annual cost opportunity before validation: £2m procurement + £1m footprint − £0.6m overlap. It is a hypothesis, not a finance-validated savings forecast. The £5m sales claim cannot be treated as EBITDA without incremental margin, cost and capacity.

## Baseline and benefit control

The last-calendar-year and trailing-12-month models are not comparable. CFO must choose and reconcile one period, scope, currency and finance baseline, with like-for-like normalization. Procurement and footprint owners should maintain a shared benefit-ID ledger: assign the £0.6m overlap once, not to both workstreams. The £2.4m is only the sum of management claims after the known overlap; additional overlap or delivery constraints can reduce it.

Separate annual gross benefit, recurring cost/disbenefit, one-off cash and monthly ramp. Each is currently missing or unvalidated, so there is no defensible net annual or first-year result. No realization probability is invented.

## 100-day evidence plan

| Workstream / accountable role | Days 0–30 | Days 31–60 | Days 61–100 | Dependency / red flag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Procurement / COO | Reconcile spend, contracts and overlap IDs with CFO | Test vendor terms and service constraints | Approve waves only with finance-validated attributable savings | Footprint volumes may change; stop on duplicate attribution or service harm |
| Footprint / Operations Director | Establish lease, labor, capacity and service baselines | Build compliant transition options and explicit one-off/running-cost schedules | Commit only after delivery and required specialist approvals | Depends on procurement and capacity; stop on safety, service or approval gaps |
| Revenue validation / Commercial Director | Validate unique opportunities and incremental margins | Pilot within capacity and measure contribution | Fund scale or stop based on evidence, not bookings alone | Capacity and acquisition costs missing; no EBITDA credit until reconciled |

CFO is sponsor and owns the ledger, monthly value review and baseline sign-off. Workstream owners review milestones/dependencies weekly. Escalate unresolved overlap, baseline conflicts, missing approvals or service risks before a commitment. At day 30, approve bounded validation work; at day 100, approve only the documented net, phased benefits with named owners and finance sign-off. Keep potential, underwritten and realized value in separate columns throughout.
