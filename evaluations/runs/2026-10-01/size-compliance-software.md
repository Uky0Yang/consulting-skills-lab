All inputs are fictional assumptions, not verified market facts.

## Reachability and next gate

At £720 × 14 = £10,080 annual revenue/customer, £4m requires ceil(4,000,000/10,080) = 397 active customers. The plan supports 80 × 3 = 240 before churn, or £2.4192m/year, leaving a 157-customer gap. This is an upper bound absent churn, ramp and sales-cycle data.

At the other base inputs, the £20m market gate needs paid adoption of 20m/(7,500 × .70 × 14 × £720) = 37.79%. That one-driver threshold does not make the joint downside safe. Strategy should validate adoption/seats with buyer evidence, quantify the supply-side scope/time bridge, and show an owned channel-capacity plan before investment approval. Neither uncertainty robustness nor obtainable revenue is demonstrated.
