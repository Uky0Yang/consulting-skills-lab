# Current Signals for Agentic AI Transformation

These notes summarize public market signals as of 2025-2026. Use them as context for executive framing, not as proof that a specific client or company has achieved value.

## Public Signals

- McKinsey's 2025 State of AI research indicates broad AI adoption, but it also emphasizes that scaling value requires workflow redesign, governance, and organizational change rather than model deployment alone.
- BCG's 2025 AI value-gap research argues that a small group of AI leaders are pulling away because they combine strategy, people, processes, technology, and value tracking.
- BCG's AI agents materials position agents as a growing source of value, especially when they are embedded into workflows with clear ownership and controls.
- Bain's 2025 technology research frames agentic AI as a transformation challenge that needs data foundations, integration patterns, governance, and redesigned ways of working.
- Industry reporting on consulting pricing suggests AI is increasing client pressure for outcome-based models. This makes value tracking and benefits realization more important in AI transformation work.
- Practitioner reporting often notes that AI adoption problems are organizational problems in disguise: unclear ownership, weak incentives, missing process redesign, and low trust.

## Implications for Skill Use

When diagnosing AI transformation, test for:

1. Value clarity: Is the initiative tied to a measurable business outcome?
2. Workflow redesign: Is the work changing, or is AI being bolted onto the old process?
3. Operating model: Who owns adoption, risk, quality, and value?
4. Controls: What decisions require human review, auditability, or escalation?
5. Scaling path: Is there a repeatable pattern or only disconnected pilots?
