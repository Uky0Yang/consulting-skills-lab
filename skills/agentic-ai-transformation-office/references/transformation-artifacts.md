## Use-Case Portfolio

| Use case | Value | Feasibility | Risk | Adoption friction | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 | 1-5 | Scale / Redesign / Pilot / Stop |

Decision logic:

- Scale: high value, clear owner, manageable controls.
- Redesign: strong value but workflow or governance gaps.
- Pilot: uncertain value but important learning.
- Stop: weak value, high risk, or no adoption path.

## Steering Meeting Pack

Use a short monthly pack:

1. Value delivered vs. target.
2. Portfolio decisions: scale, redesign, stop.
3. Top workflow blockers.
4. Risk and control exceptions.
5. Adoption metrics and manager actions.
6. Decisions needed from executives.
