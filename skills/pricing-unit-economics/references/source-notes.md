# Public method sources

Last verified: 2026-10-02. Sources define methods; they do not validate example inputs. Recheck before changing formulas or adopting a pricing-platform-specific implementation.

- [ChartMogul: CAC payback](https://chartmogul.com/saas-metrics/cac-payback/) uses monthly revenue adjusted for gross margin rather than revenue alone. This skill uses an explicitly defined contribution basis when costs differ and labels that difference.
- [Stripe: Usage-based pricing strategy for SaaS](https://stripe.com/resources/more/usage-based-pricing-strategy-for-saas) describes usage, tiered and hybrid billing approaches. Metric selection here also tests predictability and cost alignment; this is not an endorsement of a vendor or a recommendation to change a live billing system.

No external “good LTV/CAC” or payback benchmark is embedded as a universal gate. The simplified LTV proxy and volume-preservation scenarios are analytical approximations with stated assumptions, not investment valuations.
