# Diligence Workplan

Use this reference to convert a deal or market question into a rapid workplan.

## Sprint Structure

| Phase | Timing | Focus | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | Day 0-1 | Decision, thesis, kill criteria, data request | Diligence plan |
| Build fact base | Day 1-4 | Market, customer, competitor, target metrics | Evidence tracker |
| Test hypotheses | Day 3-7 | Confirm or reject thesis points | Finding pages |
| Synthesize | Day 7-10 | Investment answer, risks, value creation | IC memo |

Adjust timing for the deal clock. Keep the same logic even if the sprint is compressed.

## Interview Guide

Customer or expert interviews should test:

- Job to be done and buying trigger.
- Alternatives considered.
- Differentiation and switching costs.
- Willingness to pay and pricing pressure.
- Implementation friction or churn drivers.
- Competitor perception.
- Future demand and budget risk.

## Data Request List

Ask for:

- Revenue by product, customer segment, geography, and channel.
- Customer cohorts, retention, churn, expansion, and concentration.
- Sales pipeline, win rates, sales cycle, and acquisition cost.
- Pricing history, discounting, renewals, and gross margin.
- Product usage, support burden, and implementation timelines.
- Management forecast assumptions and historical forecast accuracy.

## Evidence Confidence

| Confidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| High | Multiple independent sources agree, and data quality is strong |
| Medium | Direction is supported, but evidence has gaps or limited triangulation |
| Low | Evidence is anecdotal, conflicting, stale, or management-supplied only |

## IC Memo Skeleton

1. Recommendation.
2. Investment thesis.
3. Market attractiveness.
4. Target competitiveness.
5. Quality of growth.
6. Value creation plan.
7. Key risks and kill criteria.
8. Evidence gaps and next diligence asks.
