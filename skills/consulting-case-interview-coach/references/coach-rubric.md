# Case Interview Coaching Rubric

Use six dimensions. Score each from 1 to 5 using observed behavior, then report the unweighted average. A critical failure in problem definition, quantitative analysis, or synthesis caps the overall level at Developing.

## Score Anchors

| Score | Observable standard |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Cannot progress without the interviewer supplying the approach; major logic or arithmetic failures remain uncorrected. |
| 2 | Shows partial competence but misses important branches, implications, or communication steps; needs repeated direction. |
| 3 | Reaches a defensible answer with minor gaps and limited prompting; performance is inconsistent but workable. |
| 4 | Drives a tailored, accurate, insight-led discussion with clear synthesis and little assistance. |
| 5 | Exceptional control: prioritizes sharply, adapts quickly, extracts non-obvious implications, and communicates like a trusted client adviser. |

## Dimensions

### Problem definition

Check whether the candidate restates the decision, objective metric, scope, constraints, and time horizon. Do not require clarification questions for their own sake.

### Structure and prioritization

Check whether branches are tailored to the client decision, collectively cover the important drivers, avoid material overlap, and identify where to start. A named framework is not evidence of quality.

### Quantitative analysis

Check equation setup, units, arithmetic, reasonableness checks, and interpretation. One corrected slip is not a major failure. Correct math without a business implication scores no higher than 3.

### Insight and business judgment

Check whether the candidate turns facts into implications, generates plausible hypotheses, considers customers and competitors, and adapts when evidence conflicts with the initial view.

### Communication and collaboration

Check signposting, concise speech, active listening, interviewer engagement, and response to feedback. Dominating the discussion or disappearing into silent calculation both reduce the score.

### Synthesis and recommendation

Check whether the conclusion answers the original question, leads with the recommendation, cites the strongest evidence, identifies material risks, and proposes a decision-relevant next step.

## Overall Levels

| Average | Level |
| ---: | --- |
| 1.0-1.9 | Not ready |
| 2.0-2.9 | Developing |
| 3.0-3.7 | Interview ready |
| 3.8-5.0 | Strong |

## Diagnostic Drills

| Weakness | Drill | Success criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic structures | Build five issue trees from prompts without solving them | Each tree is objective-linked, tailored, and names a priority branch |
| Slow math | Ten timed setup-and-calculate questions | At least 9 correct with units and a reasonableness check |
| Weak chart insight | Interpret five charts using observation, implication, action | Every answer includes a quantified observation and decision implication |
| Poor case control | Run three 10-minute openings | Restate, clarify, structure, hypothesize, and request first analysis cleanly |
| Weak synthesis | Record five 60-second recommendations | Recommendation first, two reasons, one risk, one next step |
| Framework dependence | Solve one unfamiliar public-sector or technology case | Structure from the objective without naming a stock framework |
