## Selection Guidance

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Official consulting-firm recruiting pages.
2. School or consulting-club pages that host their own material.
3. Author-controlled pages with explicit permission.
4. Third-party indexes only for discovery; follow links back to the owner.

Reject or quarantine:

- Files labeled confidential or tied to a private recruiting cohort.
- Commercial books from unofficial download sites.
- Collections with no provenance or rights statement.
- "Real interview" dumps that may expose proprietary recruiting content.

## What Is Newer Than a Classic Casebook Library

Recent source sets broaden the classic profitability and market-entry mix with:

- Climate strategy and adaptation.
- Cloud and enterprise technology migration.
- AI-enabled operations and digital products.
- Electric mobility and circular economy.
- Post-merger integration.
- Social-impact and public-sector investment.
- More explicit scoring, difficulty labels, interviewer keys, and feedback loops.

Use these themes to create new original cases. Do not translate or lightly rewrite an existing case.
