## Decision Gate

| Decision | Use when | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter | Attractive gap, reachable buyers, credible wedge | Proven or strong directional signals |
| Build | Workflow gap can be solved with product capability | Clear pain, budget, and feasible MVP |
| Partner | Distribution or credibility is the bottleneck | Complementary partner controls access |
| Invest | Market growth and target differentiation are both credible | Independent market and company evidence |
| Monitor | Signals are promising but timing is unclear | Specific trigger events to watch |
| Avoid | Weak pain, no budget, saturated category, or structural barrier | Evidence that invalidates opportunity |
