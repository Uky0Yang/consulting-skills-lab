# Market Signal Taxonomy

Use this reference to plan evidence collection and avoid overclaiming.

## Demand Signals

| Signal | What it can show | Common trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer complaints | Pain intensity and language | Vocal minority may not represent buyers |
| Search trends | Attention or discovery behavior | Attention is not willingness to pay |
| Job postings | Capability buildout and budget direction | Hiring may lag strategy or reflect one company |
| RFPs and procurement pages | Active buying motion | Public tenders may miss private demand |
| Reviews and forums | Workflow pain, switching criteria | Reviews can be biased or outdated |
| Pricing questions | Budget sensitivity | Interest may come from non-buyers |

## Supply Signals

| Signal | What it can show | Common trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New vendor launches | Category formation | Launch volume can indicate hype, not adoption |
| Funding rounds | Investor belief and capital intensity | Funding does not prove product-market fit |
| Open-source activity | Developer interest and ecosystem | Stars are not production usage |
| Partnerships | Distribution and ecosystem path | Announcements may be symbolic |
| Pricing pages | Monetization model and packaging | List price may not equal realized price |
| Hiring by vendors | Growth priorities | Hiring may reflect backlog, not demand strength |

## Behavior Signals

| Signal | What it can show | Common trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Migration stories | Switching triggers | Case studies are curated |
| Usage metrics | Adoption and depth | Vendor-reported metrics may be selective |
| Integration requests | Workflow embedding | Integrations may be table stakes |
| Community workarounds | Unserved jobs | Hobbyist needs may not be commercial |
| Churn themes | Unmet expectations | Requires triangulation |

## Constraint Signals

| Signal | What it can show | Common trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regulation | Adoption barrier or catalyst | Legal change may take years to affect buying |
| Data access | Feasibility and defensibility | Data availability differs by segment |
| Security reviews | Enterprise friction | Friction may be solvable with packaging |
| Channel dependency | Go-to-market risk | One channel can look larger than the market |
| Workflow complexity | Implementation cost | Complexity can create services revenue |

## Source Mix Rule

For each important market claim, try to combine:

1. One buyer-side signal.
2. One supplier-side signal.
3. One behavior or constraint signal.

If only supplier-side signals exist, label the claim as weak.
