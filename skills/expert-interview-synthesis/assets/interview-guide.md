## Opening

Explain purpose, voluntary participation, permitted use and recording arrangements. Confirm the participant can decline a question; do not solicit confidential third-party information.

## Neutral prompts

- Walk me through the most recent time this happened.
- What happened next, and what changed for you?
- Which records or examples support that estimate? What period and population does it cover?
- When has the opposite happened, or this approach not worked?
- What other explanation should we test?

## Close

- Paraphrase the important points for confirmation.
- Identify unanswered questions and permissible verification material.
- Explain the next use of the research; draft any follow-up separately for approval.
